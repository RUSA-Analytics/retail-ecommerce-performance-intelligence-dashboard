# Case Study: Retail & E-commerce Performance Intelligence

## Business context

Retail decision-makers often receive sales, customer, product, geography, delivery, and return information through separate reports. This fragments the operating picture and slows the identification of performance gaps.

## Demonstration objective

Build a Power BI portfolio solution that consolidates core retail and e-commerce measures into four focused decision views for executives and functional teams.

## Analytical design

The dashboard demonstrates:

- executive KPI monitoring;
- monthly and category performance analysis;
- product, channel, device, and discount comparisons;
- customer segmentation and repeat-behaviour views;
- geographic sales and profit comparisons;
- payment, delivery, order-status, and return monitoring.

## Overall results displayed

The unfiltered dashboard view displays ₹16.85M in net sales, ₹8.15M in total profit, 6,000 orders, and a 5.68% return rate. These values come from synthetic demonstration data and do not represent a real client's commercial outcome.

## Intended business use

In a real implementation, a dashboard of this type can support weekly business reviews, category and channel analysis, customer-retention discussions, regional performance reviews, and investigation of order-health exceptions.

## Limitations

- The data is synthetic.
- No client deployment, adoption, cost saving, or revenue uplift is claimed.
- Metrics must be interpreted within the report's filter context.
- The source file should not be published until final calculation and interaction QA is complete.
